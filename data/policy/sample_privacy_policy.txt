NIMBUSCART PRIVATE LIMITED — PRIVACY POLICY
(Fictional company, for demo/testing purposes only)
Last updated: January 2025

1. INTRODUCTION
NimbusCart Pvt. Ltd. ("NimbusCart", "we", "us") operates an online retail platform.
This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal data of our customers
and website visitors.

2. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT
We collect the following information when you use our platform:
- Name, email address, phone number, and shipping address
- Payment information (processed via third-party payment gateway)
- Browsing behavior and cookies on our website
- Device information (IP address, browser type)

3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA
We use your data to:
- Process and deliver your orders
- Send order confirmations and shipping updates
- Improve our website and product recommendations
- Send marketing emails about offers and new products

4. SHARING YOUR DATA
We may share your data with:
- Logistics partners for delivery purposes
- Payment gateway providers to process transactions
- Marketing service providers

5. COOKIES
Our website uses cookies to improve user experience and track browsing behavior
for analytics purposes.

6. YOUR RIGHTS
You may contact our support team at support@nimbuscart-demo.com if you have
questions about your personal data.

7. DATA SECURITY
We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from
unauthorized access.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of our platform
constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. CONTACT US
For any questions, email us at support@nimbuscart-demo.com.
